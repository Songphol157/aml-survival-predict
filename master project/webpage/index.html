About the Patient

How old the patient was when leukemia was diagnosed. Must be between 18 and 88 years.
The patient's biological sex.
A risk category from lab and genetic testing that shows how the leukemia is expected to respond to treatment: Favorable is the best outlook, Adverse is the most difficult. Your doctor's report will state this.
"De novo" means the leukemia appeared without a prior cancer. "Therapy-related" means it developed after chemotherapy or radiation for a different, earlier condition.
From a test called karyotyping. "Normal" means no major chromosome changes were found. "Complex" means several chromosome abnormalities were found together.
Genetic Mutation Test Results

These are individual genes commonly tested for in AML using a blood or bone marrow sample. For each one, choose Positive if the mutation was found, Negative if it was tested for and not found, or Unknown if that test wasn't done or you're not sure.

Results will appear after prediction.
